Questions

Everything you might ask.

Is NewsLearner free?

Yes — it's free to download and start reading on iPhone and Android. An optional NewsLearner Pro subscription removes ads and unlocks unlimited AI article adaptation and grammar help.

Which languages can I learn?

Seven: English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese and Russian. Switch any time — your saved words and streak come with you.

How is it different from apps like Duolingo?

Instead of invented sentences, NewsLearner teaches you with real, current news articles — the same stories native speakers read. You pick up vocabulary in genuine context, which is what makes it stick.

What does "adapt to your level" mean?

NewsLearner's AI can rewrite any article from A1 (beginner) to C1 (advanced), so the same story is readable whether you're just starting out or nearly fluent.

Does it work offline?

Your saved words and all four review modes work offline — practice on a plane or the subway. Loading new articles needs a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no email and no password. Your saved words and progress stay on your device.

Is it available on Android?

Yes — NewsLearner is on Google Play now, and on the App Store for iPhone. Both versions have the same features.
